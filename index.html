RESEARCH

Research Areas

ISPL investigates signal-centric intelligence for complex engineering systems, with emphasis on multichannel sensing, physics-guided processing, fault diagnostics, and predictive maintenance.

01

Intelligent Signal Processing

Advanced signal representations, feature enhancement, noise suppression, and data-driven analysis.

02

Multichannel Sensing & Sensor Fusion

Fusion of distributed and multi-axis measurements for robust condition monitoring.

03

Physics-Guided Beamforming

Physics-informed steering and spatial filtering for weak fault-signal enhancement.

04

Machinery Diagnostics

Signal-based fault detection and diagnosis for rotating machinery and precision transmission systems.

05

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning and deep learning for intelligent interpretation of engineering signals.

06

PHM

Prognostics and health management for reliable, condition-aware industrial systems.
